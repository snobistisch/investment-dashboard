Who captures the rent when software starts acting?

Four listed businesses express distinct parts of the agent stack: compute, banking distribution, banking middleware and programmable card issuing. The shared uncertainty is whether agent adoption becomes measurable revenue rather than product language.

01 · Research map

Four companies, one adoption question

NU

Nu Holdings

Consumer financial distribution · lower thesis dependence

More than 100 million customers, verified identity, transaction history and a banking licence form a plausible home for a personal financial agent. The underlying bank already operates without the agent thesis.

Watch Brazilian macro conditions, credit performance and whether announced AI strategy becomes a shipped consumer product.

SOFI

SoFi Technologies

Banking middleware · platform bet

Galileo has shipped Cyberbank Konecta AI agents into production. That makes the thesis observable in an operating product instead of only a roadmap.

Watch the credit cycle, customer concentration and competition from Stripe, Marqeta and Unit.

CRWV

CoreWeave

Compute infrastructure · thesis-dependent optionality

A large contracted backlog is real evidence of demand for specialised compute. The unproven step is whether latency-sensitive inference earns a durable premium over hyperscaler capacity.

Watch customer concentration, financing needs and any standard-priced hyperscaler inference tier that erodes the claimed premium.

MQ

Marqeta

Programmable card issuing · lower thesis dependence

Real-time card issuance, spending controls and transaction-level authorisation already sit underneath modern fintech products. Autonomous purchasing can add volume without requiring Marqeta to own the end-user agent.

Watch Block/Cash App concentration and competition from Stripe Issuing, Adyen and Unit.

02 · What would update the thesis

Evidence before valuation

03 · Portfolio reading

Owning four names is not four independent bets

All four depend on adoption arriving on a commercially useful timetable. NU and SOFI share financial-distribution risk; CRWV supplies the compute; MQ supplies transaction infrastructure. Size the common adoption factor before treating company differences as diversification.
